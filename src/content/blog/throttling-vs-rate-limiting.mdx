Throttling and rate limiting are often used as synonyms, but they answer
different questions: rate limiting decides *whether* a request is allowed in a
window, while throttling decides *how fast* work is allowed to proceed. This post
will build toward when to reach for each — and how they compose into a resilient
API — rather than declaring a winner up front.
